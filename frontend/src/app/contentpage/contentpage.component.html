About Us

e-Gram Panchayat is a web-based portal that provides e-governance services to the citizens of the Gram Panchayat. It is a one-stop destination for all e-governance services in the Gram Panchayat.

E-Gram Panchayat Services is a digital platform designed to make local government services more accessible, convenient, and efficient for citizens. The system provides a centralized online platform where citizens can access various Gram Panchayat services without having to visit the Panchayat office for every requirement. The platform allows citizens to register and log in securely, apply for different certificates, make tax and service payments, track application status, and receive important updates regarding their requests. Services such as Birth Certificate, Death Certificate, Marriage Certificate, Income Certificate, and Caste Certificate can be managed through the online system. E-Gram Panchayat also provides an administrative interface for Panchayat officials to manage citizen applications, verify submitted information and documents, process service requests, manage payments, and update application statuses. The main objective of the project is to reduce manual paperwork, minimize unnecessary visits to government offices, improve transparency, and provide faster access to Panchayat services. By bringing essential local government services into a single digital platform, E-Gram Panchayat aims to create a more convenient and citizen-friendly experience.
